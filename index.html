Our Team

The People Behind The Practice

Psychomotricity came to the United Kingdom in 2016. The clinicians, directors and visiting teachers below are the reason it took root, and the reason it is now taught, practised and researched here.

Direction & Founding
Mr Karmen Mpia Kasai
Founder & Director
Consultant in psychomotricity and relaxation with more than ten years' experience, working across the NHS and the private sector within women's and children's health. His main interest is fetal medicine, its preventive application and practice, and fetal surgery intervention in low-income settings. He is developing an antenatal database to identify babies at risk of autism, or likely to need special educational support later, so that intervention can begin early.
Prof. Boniface M’Burling Kasai
Co-director · Chief Neuromodulation Officer
Speech pathologist. Pioneer of psychomotricity in sub-Saharan Africa, and co-founder of the Institut Panafricain de Psychomotricité et de Relaxation (IPPR). His method of psychomotor rehabilitation for psychosomatic pain has been carried to patients across three continents.
Prof. Dr. Franco Boscaini
Co-director · CISERPP Verona
Director of CISERPP Verona. He opened our founding seminar in 2016 and supervised the conferral of the first British psychomotricians in 2026. At the Centre he leads the training of students and directs the clinical multidisciplinary team.
Prof. Dr. Silvia Cattafesta
Co-director · CISERPP Verona
President of the European Forum of Psychomotricity. She leads the training of students alongside Prof. Boscaini and directs the clinical multidisciplinary team, bringing European standards of the profession into everyday practice in London.

Our Approach

Advanced Therapy Modalities

Psychomotricity forms the core of our model — an evidence-based discipline that unites body, mind, and movement. We integrate the world's most advanced technologies and therapeutic approaches within this holistic framework.

01
Psychomotricity
Our founding discipline integrating movement, sensory processing, and neurological development as a unified therapeutic system.
Core Model
02
Tomatis & Sound Therapy
Auditory retraining using high-precision frequency treatment to rewire neural pathways for language, focus and emotional regulation.
Neuro-Sound
03
TPS — Transcranial Pulse
Ultrasound-based brain stimulation targeting the TPJ, mPFC and cerebellar networks for social skills, sleep and emotional balance.
Technology
04
tDCS & taVNS
Non-invasive neurostimulation via transcranial direct current and transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation for cognition and mood.
Neuromodulation
05
HBOT
Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy delivering pressurised oxygen to accelerate neural repair and reduce inflammation.
Oxygen Therapy
06
Piano Cognitive Therapy
Music-based memory enhancement and cognitive stimulation using structured piano exercises for neuroplasticity.
Music & Cognition
07
Gut & Microbiome Therapy
Gut-brain axis optimisation including dietary protocols and, where clinically indicated, fecal microbiota transplantation for severe cases.
Gut-Brain Axis
08
Art, Clay & Reflexology
Expressive therapies — art, clay and music — combined with reflexology and sensory integration for holistic neurological engagement.
Expressive Therapy
09
Laser & Cryotherapy
Low-level laser therapy and high-pressure medical cryotherapy for neuro and sport injury recovery in adults.
Physical Recovery
