Our Approach

Advanced Therapy Modalities

Psychomotricity forms the core of our model — an evidence-based discipline that unites body, mind, and movement. We integrate the world's most advanced technologies and therapeutic approaches within this holistic framework.

01
Psychomotricity
Our founding discipline integrating movement, sensory processing, and neurological development as a unified therapeutic system.
Core Model
02
Tomatis & Sound Therapy
Auditory retraining using high-precision frequency treatment to rewire neural pathways for language, focus and emotional regulation.
Neuro-Sound
03
TPS — Transcranial Pulse
Ultrasound-based brain stimulation targeting the TPJ, mPFC and cerebellar networks for social skills, sleep and emotional balance.
Technology
04
tDCS & taVNS
Non-invasive neurostimulation via transcranial direct current and transcutaneous vagus nerve stimulation for cognition and mood.
Neuromodulation
05
HBOT
Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy delivering pressurised oxygen to accelerate neural repair and reduce inflammation.
Oxygen Therapy
06
Piano Cognitive Therapy
Music-based memory enhancement and cognitive stimulation using structured piano exercises for neuroplasticity.
Music & Cognition
07
Gut & Microbiome Therapy
Gut-brain axis optimisation including dietary protocols and, where clinically indicated, fecal microbiota transplantation for severe cases.
Gut-Brain Axis
08
Art, Clay & Reflexology
Expressive therapies — art, clay and music — combined with reflexology and sensory integration for holistic neurological engagement.
Expressive Therapy
09
Laser & Cryotherapy
Low-level laser therapy and high-pressure medical cryotherapy for neuro and sport injury recovery in adults.
Physical Recovery
